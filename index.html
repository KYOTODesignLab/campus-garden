About

A garden is never finished.

Campus Garden is an experimental garden at Kyoto Institute of Technology. Students, gardeners, faculty, and visitors continue to shape it through making, use, care, and observation.

The project brings hands-on knowledge together with digital tools. From on-site construction supported by XR to environmental sensing, photography, mapping, and 3D recording, each experiment becomes part of the garden's evolving record and informs what comes next.

Each form of evidence — physical and digital — offers a different way to understand how the site changes through seasons, growth, use, and care.

This website is an evolving project archive. Its structure and records will continue to develop as new observations and work are added — the record grows with the garden rather than closing around it.
