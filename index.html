About

A garden is never finished.

Campus Garden is an experimental garden at Kyoto Institute of Technology. Students, gardeners, faculty, and visitors continue to shape it through making, use, care, and observation.

The project brings hands-on knowledge together with digital tools — construction, environmental sensing, photography, mapping, and 3D recording. Each activity and record is shared to deepen understanding of the garden and support what happens next.

Place

A courtyard garden on the Matsugasaki campus, Kyoto Institute of Technology.

Project

An experimental garden held as a living, shared record rather than a finished result.

Workshop

Developed across phases: design & construction, then archive, maintenance, and connection.

Methods

Body-led judgement joined with XR, sensing, photography, mapping, and 3D scanning.
